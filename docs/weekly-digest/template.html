Drop-offs to watch

Metric · persistence · observation · hypothesis
How to read this section. Each drop-off is logged with an observation (what we see) and a hypothesis (why we think it's happening). Next week's report re-runs the same drop-offs — if the metric stays elevated the persistence tag advances (Week 2 → Week 3 → Persisting), and hypotheses get sharpened or replaced. Anything that clears returns tagged "Resolved". Use this as the running audit trail for what to fine-tune on the site.
−31%
New this week
Q2 → Q3 abandonment · Timing question
Observation

7 of 24 concierge starts dropped between Q2 (Pressure) and Q3 (Timing). Prior three weeks averaged −18% at this step, so this jumped ~13pp above baseline.

Hypothesis

The Timing question forces a date commit before some users are ready. If they don't have a fixed date yet, they may abandon rather than pick an approximate answer. Test: add an explicit "Not sure yet" option and re-measure next week.

54%
Week 3 · Stable
Recommendation → Book CTA · click-through
Observation

54% of users who received a recommendation clicked "Book a Discovery Call". Ratio has held ±3pp across three consecutive weeks — this is now the baseline, not the anomaly.

Hypothesis

The recommendation copy lands, but roughly half aren't call-ready. They want the info, not the meeting. Test: add a soft-intent secondary CTA — "Email me this brief" — to capture the delta without forcing a call commit.

−33%
New this week
Book CTA click → zcal confirm
Observation

33% of Book CTA clicks did not finish on zcal (2 of 6). Prior week the drop-off was 22%. Small denominator so treat cautiously, but the direction is worth flagging.

Hypothesis

zcal's next-available slots this week were Thu-Fri only. Users scanning for something sooner may bounce when they see limited availability. Test: open 2-3 extra morning slots in your zcal, revisit next week.

Worth noticing

The four things that stood out this week
01
Concierge starts up 45% WoW

Correlates with the LinkedIn Insight-Tag audience going live 26 Nov. Direct-traffic sessions that started the concierge doubled — worth pushing more warm LinkedIn traffic once the audience matures.

02
Dead clicks cluster on Concierge submit

10 of 12 dead clicks are on the Concierge submit button before the fourth answer is selected. Users are trying to submit early. Suggest a disabled-state style + "answer all 4 to continue" hint.

03
FAQ Q3 opened 3.2× more than any other Q

"How fast do you turn around a proposal?" — 38 opens vs the next-highest at 12. If this holds two more weeks, consider surfacing the answer above the fold as a hero sub-line.

04
Malaysia traffic up 2.5× — one referral

Traceable to a single LinkedIn post shared by Zafira Rahman (BrainHack 2026 alum). Small numbers but a clean signal that individual reshares move measurable traffic. Worth reaching out.
